# Grooming and hygiene

## Brushing frequency
Short-haired dogs and cats generally need brushing once a week. Long-haired or double-coated pets such as Shiba Inus, huskies, collies and Persian cats benefit from brushing several times a week, and daily during seasonal shedding.

## Bathing
Most dogs need a bath every 4 to 8 weeks, or when dirty, using a pet-safe shampoo. Most cats rarely need baths. Over-bathing can dry out skin.

## Nail trimming
Trim nails every 3 to 4 weeks. If you can hear nails clicking on the floor, they are likely too long.

## Dental care
Brush a dog's or cat's teeth several times a week, ideally daily, using pet-safe toothpaste. Dental disease is common and affects overall health.

## Ear checks
Check ears weekly for redness, odor or discharge, particularly in floppy-eared dogs. Report persistent problems to a vet.
