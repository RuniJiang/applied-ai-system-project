# Cat care
species: cat

## Adult cat feeding
Adult cats do best with two measured meals a day, for example 7:30 and 18:00, or with portion-controlled feeding throughout the day. Cats need constant access to fresh water. Wet food helps hydration.

## Kitten feeding
Kittens under 6 months need three to four small meals a day of food formulated for kittens. Move to two to three meals as they approach one year.

## Cat play and enrichment
Cats benefit from two or three short play sessions a day of 10 to 15 minutes each, using wand toys or chase toys, ideally before meals to mimic hunting. Rotate toys and offer climbing spaces and scratching posts for enrichment.

## Litter box care
Scoop the litter box at least once a day and fully change the litter weekly. Keep one box per cat plus one extra. Changes in litter box habits can signal illness and are worth mentioning to a vet.

## Senior cat care
Cats aged 10 and older benefit from gentle play of 5 to 10 minutes, easy access to litter boxes and beds, and vet checkups every 6 months. Watch for weight loss, increased thirst and changes in grooming.
