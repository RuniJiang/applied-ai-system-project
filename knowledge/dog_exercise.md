# Dog exercise
species: dog

## Adult dog daily exercise
Most healthy adult dogs need 30 to 60 minutes of exercise per day, often split into two walks. High-energy breeds such as border collies, huskies and retrievers may need 90 minutes or more. Small companion breeds such as Shiba Inus, pugs and bulldogs usually do well with 30 to 45 minutes of walking plus play.

## Senior dog exercise
Dogs aged 8 and older (earlier for giant breeds) benefit from shorter, gentler walks of 15 to 25 minutes, two or three times a day, instead of one long outing. Avoid hard running and jumping. Consistent low-impact movement helps maintain muscle and joint mobility. Watch for limping, lagging behind or stiffness after rest, and mention these to a vet.

## Puppy exercise
Puppies under 12 months need short, frequent play and walks. A common guideline is about 5 minutes of structured exercise per month of age, up to twice a day. Avoid forced running on hard surfaces while bones are still developing. Short training sessions of 5 to 10 minutes also provide mental exercise.

## Mental enrichment for dogs
Dogs benefit from 10 to 20 minutes of daily mental enrichment such as puzzle feeders, sniff walks, training practice or fetch. Enrichment helps reduce boredom-related behavior such as chewing and barking.
