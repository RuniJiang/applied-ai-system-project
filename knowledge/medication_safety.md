# Medication and health safety

## Preventive care schedule
Many pets receive monthly flea, tick and heartworm preventives. Set a recurring monthly or weekly reminder on a fixed day, matching the product label and your vet's instructions. Annual vet checkups are recommended for adult pets and every 6 months for seniors.

## Medication dosing
Medication dosing depends on a pet's weight, age, health and the specific product. Never guess a dose or give human medications such as ibuprofen, acetaminophen or aspirin to pets, because many are toxic. Always confirm drug, dose and timing with a veterinarian.

## Giving medication on time
Give prescribed medication at the same times each day, with or without food as the vet directs. A recurring high-priority task with a reminder helps prevent missed doses.

## When to call a vet
Contact a vet promptly for vomiting or diarrhea lasting more than a day, loss of appetite, lethargy, difficulty breathing, limping or suspected poisoning.
