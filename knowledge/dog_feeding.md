# Dog feeding
species: dog

## Adult dog feeding schedule
Adult dogs are usually fed twice a day, about 8 to 12 hours apart, for example breakfast at 7:00 or 8:00 and dinner at 17:00 or 18:00. Feeding at consistent times supports digestion and makes bathroom breaks predictable. Always provide fresh water.

## Puppy feeding schedule
Puppies under 6 months typically eat three to four small meals a day. After 6 months most can move to two meals a day. Use food labeled for puppies, and keep meal times consistent.

## Senior dog feeding
Senior dogs often need fewer calories and may benefit from food formulated for older dogs. Two smaller meals a day are common. A vet may recommend joint-support supplements; follow veterinary guidance on any supplement and never choose a dose without a vet.
