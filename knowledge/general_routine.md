# General routine planning

## Spacing tasks
Avoid scheduling two care tasks at the same start time for one owner. Leave at least 15 minutes between tasks so each can be done properly.

## Walk timing
Walk dogs before meals rather than right after, and avoid vigorous exercise within an hour after eating. In hot weather, walk early in the morning or in the evening.

## Priorities
Medication, feeding and bathroom breaks are high priority. Exercise and grooming are medium priority. Enrichment and play are usually low priority but still important for wellbeing.
